# UDY Healthcare corporate website

## Goal
Build a complete five-page corporate website for UDY Healthcare Private Limited using the supplied logo and real product materials, with no invented claims or contact details.

## Visual direction
- Use the logo unchanged and derive the complete interface palette from its sampled blue and aqua colors, supported only by white and restrained neutral text/borders.
- Present a clean, precise pharmaceutical identity with modern sans-serif typography, generous white space, thin lines, restrained shadows, and modest corner radii.
- Keep product packaging visually authentic and dominant; use polished pack shots for products and hero imagery, and supplied photography for the gallery.

## Pages and navigation
- Add a shared responsive header, mobile menu, and footer linking Home, About Us, Products, Gallery, and Contact Us.
- Build the homepage in the requested order: hero, company preview, healthcare segments, featured products, business process, quality, product showcase, gallery preview, and contact call-to-action.
- Build dedicated About, Products, Gallery, and Contact pages with unique metadata and factual content.

## Products and imagery
- Extract and optimize the 35 polished product pages as website imagery, preserving every label and package proportion.
- Create structured product data using exact product names; add composition or dosage form only where legible and confirmed.
- Use the 15 supplied photography pages for a responsive gallery with an accessible lightbox.
- Add understated filtering only for categories that the supplied information clearly supports, plus the required medical disclaimer.

## Interaction and validation
- Add subtle section entrances and restrained image/card movement, respecting reduced-motion preferences.
- Implement a keyboard-accessible mobile menu, product details, gallery lightbox, and responsive business-process timeline.
- Implement the contact form with required, email, and phone validation plus clear success/error feedback; keep submission local until verified contact handling is supplied.

## Accuracy, accessibility, and verification
- Use only supplied company facts, approved wording, product assets, and claims.
- Do not display missing phone, email, WhatsApp, social links, certifications, medical advice, pricing, or commerce controls.
- Add factual Organization structured data and unique SEO metadata, canonical links, Open Graph fields, and Twitter cards per page.
- Verify navigation, desktop/mobile layouts, image fidelity, keyboard behavior, form states, and the absence of overflow or browser errors.

## Technical details
- Keep the existing TanStack Start, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind v4 foundation rather than replacing the fixed project framework.
- Build reusable layout, card, timeline, gallery, modal, and form components with semantic design tokens in the global stylesheet.
- Store uploaded PDF-derived media through the project asset flow and lazy-load non-critical images.
